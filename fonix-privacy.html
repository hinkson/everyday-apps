Privacy Policy

Fonix is built to be private by design — no user accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking, and no data sale.

Information the app stores

Fonix stores only the game data you create on your device: which puzzles you've solved, your streaks and stats, your hint usage, your chosen color theme, and your Fonix Plus purchase status.

Where that information stays

This information is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to a server and is not shared with the developer.

Information collected by the developer

The app does not collect personal information from you. If you email support, the information you choose to include in that message is used only to respond to you.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

Fonix does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking technologies, and does not sell personal data.

Purchases

Fonix Plus is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. Fonix does not see or store your payment information. Purchase validation is performed on your device using Apple's StoreKit.

Notifications

Any reminders the app offers are scheduled locally on your device and can be turned off at any time in Settings and in your device's system settings.

Backups and Apple services

Your device settings, iCloud backup settings, and App Store activity are handled by Apple according to Apple's own policies. Fonix does not control those services.

Children's privacy

Fonix is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. If data practices change materially, this page will be updated before the new version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email support.altaaffirmations@gmail.com.
