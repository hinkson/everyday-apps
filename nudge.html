Nudge

A calendar that sets your reminders for you. Tell it once how far ahead you need warning, and it adds every one of those reminders to every new event by itself, including the events you make somewhere else.

A week to plan. A day to prepare. An hour to wrap up. Fifteen minutes to actually go. No calendar will do all four for you, so you add them by hand until you stop bothering and start missing things.

Coming soon to the App Store

Your reminders, applied for you

Set your lead times once. Every new event gets all of them, without you touching a thing.

Every calendar you have

Nudge builds on the calendar already on your iPhone, so it sees whatever you added in Settings, from whichever service. It needs no login of its own.

Alerts that outlive the app

The alerts go on the events themselves. They show up in the Calendar app, they fire on your Mac and your Watch, and they keep working even if you delete Nudge.

A full calendar

Month, week, day and list views. Type "lunch with Sam Thursday at 1" and get an event.

Boards for what is not an event

Columns of task cards you drag between, each holding steps, a due date and the same reminder set your events get. Any line of a board's notes becomes a task with one tap.

Widgets

Up Next shows your next events and how many alerts each one carries. Due Soon shows the tasks coming up. Part of Plus.

Honest about its limits

Some events cannot take every alert. An event that repeats daily cannot carry a reminder set a week ahead, and some accounts cap how many alerts an event may have. Nudge fits what it can and says what it dropped and why, rather than failing quietly.

Is Nudge working?

A screen listing every event Nudge has touched and every one it could not, with the reason. And a single button that removes every alert Nudge ever added, leaving anything you set yourself exactly as it was.

Free to start

The whole promise is free: one set of up to four reminders on every new event, plus a board with unlimited tasks and steps. Plus is a one-time purchase, never a subscription itself, and adds a different set per calendar, rules for events that need special treatment, unlimited boards, widgets, leave by alerts and extra themes.

Private by design

Your calendar never leaves your device. No account, no server, no analytics. It talks to nothing.
