How do I play?

Each puzzle is a real word spelled a strange new way, where every chunk of letters makes a sound it makes in other words. Read the scramble out loud and you'll usually hear the answer. Stuck? Use a hint.

Quick answers

Is my progress stored anywhere?
Only on your device. Nothing is sent to a server or the developer.
What's free vs. Fonix Plus?
You can sound out 10 puzzles in every difficulty for free, plus a daily puzzle. Fonix Plus is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited puzzles, unlimited hints, full stats, and extra themes.
I bought Plus but it's not showing.
Open Settings in the app and tap “Restore purchases.” If it still doesn't appear, email us.
A puzzle seems wrong or unfair.
Send us the scramble and the answer. We review reports and refine the puzzle set.
