Privacy Policy

Dues is built to be private by design: no user accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking, and no data sale.

Information the app stores

Dues stores the subscription information you enter (names, prices, currencies, billing cycles and renewal dates) along with your reminder and appearance preferences.

Where that information stays

This information is stored locally on your device. To power the Home-Screen widget, it is shared with the widget through a private app group on the same device. It is not sent to a server and is not shared with the developer.

Information collected by the developer

The app does not collect personal information from you. If you email support, the information you choose to include in that message is used only to respond to you.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

Dues does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking technologies, and does not sell personal data.

Purchases

The optional one-time upgrade is handled by Apple through the App Store. Dues does not see or store your payment information; purchase validation is performed on your device using Apple's StoreKit.

Notifications

Renewal reminders are scheduled locally on your device and can be turned off at any time in Settings and in your device's system settings.

Backups and exports

If you export a backup, the file is created on your device and goes wherever you choose to save or share it. Your device settings, iCloud backup settings, and App Store activity are handled by Apple according to Apple's own policies.

Children's privacy

Dues is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. If data practices change materially, this page will be updated before the new version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email support.altaaffirmations@gmail.com.
