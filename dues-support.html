Quick answers

Where is my subscription data stored?
Locally on your device (and shared privately with the widget on the same device). Nothing is sent to a server or the developer.
Why aren't reminders showing up?
Make sure notifications are enabled for Dues in the iOS Settings app, and that a reminder is turned on for the subscription.
How do I back up or move my list?
Use the export option to save a backup file you control, then import it on another device.
I upgraded but the extra features aren't showing.
Open Settings and tap “Restore purchases.” If it still doesn't appear, email us.

Good details to include

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • Which screen had the issue.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What happened instead.
