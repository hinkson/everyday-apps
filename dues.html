Dues

Every subscription in one place, so you never get surprise-charged again. See what renews when, and what you actually spend each month.

Download on the App Store

Reminders, trials included

Get a heads-up before each renewal. Mark a free trial with the day it ends, and Dues reminds you 2 days before it becomes a charge, with what that charge will be.

Cancel faster

For 30 popular services, open the subscription and tap Cancel to go straight to that service's own cancel or manage page.

Widgets, free for everyone

Your monthly total, your year month by month, and what renews next, on your Home Screen or your Lock Screen. A trial shows the day it ends. Tap any renewal to open it in Dues.

Clear spending

See your real monthly and yearly totals across everything you pay for. When a price changes, Dues keeps the old one, so "was $17.99" sits under the new price.

Fast to add

Search common services or add your own, with the currency you use.

Your data, exportable

Back up and restore your list as a simple file you control.

Free to start

Track up to 10 subscriptions free, with trial reminders, cancel links and price history included. Plus is a one-time $2.99 (never a subscription itself) and adds unlimited tracking and spending insights by category.

Private by design

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.
